About me

I’m Benni, a mathematician who recently completed a PhD in Edinburgh and has started working on AI safety. I spend most of my time untangling complex issues and building systems that work for people.

I’m driven by two desires: to understand and to help. The first led me to study physics and maths, and keeps motivating me to learn and to grow. The second has come to matter more and more: I want to help others and to work together towards shared goals. Over the last few years, I have built support structures for week-long academies, and more recently I have turned to AI safety, where the two come together.

I’m currently looking for my next role in AI safety research or in work that combines careful analytical thinking with supporting people. If that sounds like something you’re working on, get in touch.

I also play the piano, the double bass, and video games, and I sing, climb, dance, and read.

Mathematics

I completed my PhD in mathematics at the University of Edinburgh in 2026, supervised by Tudor Dimofte. My research is on topological quantum field theories (TQFTs) and generalised symmetries, in particular on when and how these symmetries can be gauged.

Before Edinburgh, I studied physics at Heidelberg University, where I wrote my Master’s thesis on higher gauge theory.

Papers

  • 2026
    Gauging 0-form, 1-form and 2-group symmetries in Reshetikhin–Turaev theories

    arXiv preprint. Develops tools for computing examples and connects the earlier construction to zesting, with examples including Tambara–Yamagami categories, Dijkgraaf–Witten gauge theories and anomalies in Chern–Simons theory.

  • 2025
    2-Group Symmetries of 3-dimensional Defect TQFTs and Their Gauging

    With Nils Carqueville. arXiv preprint. Shows that these symmetries can be gauged to give new TQFTs exactly when certain defects satisfy the axioms of orbifold data.

Community and mental health

Community and mental health have been essential throughout my experiences in orchestras, in academia and among friends. Since my first academy in 2015, the CdE has been one of the places where I have found a lasting sense of community. I have volunteered much of my time building systems that support people at our events, whether with organisational problems, personal and mental health issues, or interpersonal conflicts. I have also completed training as a mental health first aider.

What I do

  • 2022–now
    Volunteer management, CdE academies

    Set up and coordinate two volunteer teams at 20 events so far: a first point of contact for any issue, often mental health or interpersonal, and an on-call emergency team. This includes training volunteers in conversation skills and emergency protocols, and raising mental health awareness.

  • 2022–now
    Feedback, Awareness, Complaints and Intervention Teams, CdE

    Leading roles in developing and running systems that foster inclusivity and safer spaces, with guidelines for organisers and course instructors and support for participants during and after events.

  • 2025
    Certified Mental Health First Aider

    MHFA training, completed online.

  • 2024–25
    Organiser, CdE Winter Academy 2024/25

    Assigned courses, rooms, materials and accommodation using a preferential voting system, managed the inventory, and arranged individual needs with course instructors and participants.

Organisations

  • CdE, the alumni association of the Deutsche SchülerAkademie, which runs the week-long academies I volunteer at
  • Mental Health First Aid England and MHFA Ersthelfer in Germany, who train mental health first aiders
  • iThrive Edinburgh, information on mental health and wellbeing services in Edinburgh, run by the charity Health in Mind
